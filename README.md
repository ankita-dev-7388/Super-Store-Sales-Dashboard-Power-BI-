# Super-Store-Sales-Dashboard-Power-BI-
An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyses Super Store sales and profit across regions, customer segments, product categories, shipping modes and payment methods, plus a 15-day sales forecast.

---

##Project Overview
The goal of this project is to turn raw retail transaction data into a clear, interactive view of business performance, so a manager can quickly see:
- How much the store sells and earns, and how that changes month to month
- Which regions, states, categories and sub-categories drive the most revenue
- Which customer segments and payment modes matter most
- How shipping choices are distributed
- What sales are likely to look like in the next 15 days

## Dashboard Features

### Page 1 – Super Store Sales Dashboard

- Region slicer (Central / East / South / West) that filters the whole page
- KPI cards: Orders, Sales, Profit, Ship Days
- Donut charts: Sales by Payment Mode, Region and Segment
- Area charts: Sales by Month and Profit by Month, compared by year (2019 vs 2020)
- Bar charts: Sales by Ship Mode, Category and Sub-Category
- Map: Profit and Sales by State

### Page 2 – 15-Day Sales Forecast

- Daily sales history with a 15-day forecast and confidence band
- Zoomed-in view of recent sales with a date range slider
- Top 10 states by sales
